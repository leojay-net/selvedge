This is Selvedge, a quick tutorial. Selvedge lets AI agents spend money on Solana, but only inside rules you set. Let's walk through the site, then the Android app.

The landing page tells the story. Meet Scout, an agent with a wallet. Scroll down, and you see the problem: one hidden sentence on a web page tells Scout to send money to a stranger, and Scout obeys.

The fix is a mandate. On the left, you write the rules: a daily spending cap, the payees Scout may pay, and a switch to revoke it all.

Now try to break it. Click "Send funds to a stranger". Watch it travel through the gates. The engine policy stops it at once: the payee is not on the list.

Tick "Compromise the agent", so Scout skips its own checks, and attack again. This time the mandate program on Solana refuses it. The money never moves.

Ask for unlimited access, and the third gate takes over: a dangerous transaction needs your fingerprint on the phone. Try every attack, and the scoreboard awards the vault badge.

Further down is the proof. These numbers are read live from devnet, next to the Kani proofs and test counts. The refusal you just watched has a real transaction on chain.

Open Agents to see every live mandate on devnet, with its cap, what it has spent, and its status. Click one for the full detail.

Each mandate shows its rules, its payees, and a kill switch. With your wallet connected, one click revokes it.

Activity is the security review. Paste any transaction, and Selvedge decodes it, simulates it, and explains what it would do before you sign.

Containment is the record of a real agent run. A hidden instruction tried to pay a stranger, and each refusal shows which gate stopped it.

Now the phone. Get the app only downloads on an Android phone. On a desktop it tells you to open the page on your phone.

On Android, open Selvedge and choose a wallet. On the emulator we use the test wallet, which the app clearly labels as a software key.

Tap New agent, name it, and set its cap. Scout now appears in your agent list with its mandate, marked safe.

Open the agent to edit its mandate: the cap, the period, the expiry, the allowed programs and payees.

Then review a transaction. Paste it, and tap Review. This one hands a stranger unlimited access to your tokens, so Selvedge shows a red danger warning.

The only way past this screen is your fingerprint. This phone has none enrolled, so the transaction stays blocked.

That's Selvedge: agents that can only do what you allowed. It's open source, and every transaction you saw is on devnet.
