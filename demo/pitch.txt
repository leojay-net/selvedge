AI agents are starting to pay for things on their own: data, compute, and other agents' services. On Solana, with x402, a payment takes a second. But today you have two bad choices.

Give the agent your keys, and one poisoned web page can drain your wallet. Or approve every payment yourself, and the agent is no longer autonomous.

Selvedge is the third choice. You give each agent a mandate on Solana: how much it may spend, who it may pay, and for how long. The agent works freely inside it, and nothing outside it can happen, even if the agent is fully compromised.

That unlocks agents for real money: people handing chores to an assistant, teams running procurement and trading bots, and services that sell to agents over x402. They get the speed of autonomy without giving away the keys.

Three independent gates enforce it: a policy check in the agent, the mandate program on Solana, proven with Kani, and your fingerprint on your phone for anything dangerous. Let me show you.

Now compromise the agent so it skips that check, and attack again. The program on Solana still refuses. The money never moves.

Ask for unlimited access, and the third gate demands your fingerprint. Overspend, replay an old payment, or pay after the mandate is revoked: every single attack fails, and nothing leaves the vault.
